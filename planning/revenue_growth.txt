**WHAT**

Revenue increased sharply between September and November in both 2010 and 2011, followed by a decline in December, suggesting a recurring seasonal pattern. Orders and sales volume declined after November. Between September and November, there was a significant increase in both sales volume and revenue. This increase was largely driven by the United Kingdom, which contributed 84.82% of total revenue. We also observed an increase in revenue and sales volume across all products during this peak period.

However, I would request additional data to further investigate the underlying causes of the revenue spike during this period.

Understanding these patterns is important for inventory planning, demand forecasting, and resource allocation.
