Online Retail Portfolio Project: Stakeholder Questions

1. Sales Manager:
How did sales performance change between 2009–2010 and 2010–2011 in UK, and what months contributed most to the change?

2. Inventory / Merchandising Manager:
Which products drive the most revenue and sales volume, and which products may require a review of their inventory strategy?

3. Customer Relationship Manager:
How much of the business comes from repeat customers, and which customer segments contribute the most revenue?

4. International Sales Manager:
Which countries contribute the most revenue, and which international markets show potential for expansion?

5. Operations / Commercial Manager:
When does customer demand peak, and how can the business use purchasing patterns to improve seasonal planning
